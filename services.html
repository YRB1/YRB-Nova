From Booking to Launch

What happens after you book

A typical project, start to finish. Exact timing depends on your package and how quickly we get your content.

  1. 1

    BookingChoose a package

    Pay securely online, or book a free call first if you have questions. You get an email confirmation straight away.

  2. 2

    Day 1Discovery call

    A short call about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do. You send your logo, photos and any text you have.

  3. 3

    Days 2-3Design & build

    Your site is designed and built from scratch, then tested on phones, tablets and laptops.

  4. 4

    Days 3-4Your feedback

    You review the site and tell us what to change. Standard and Premium include revisions until you're happy.

  5. 5

    Days 2-5Launch

    Your site goes live with on-page SEO in place. Add optional hosting & support (£10/month or £100/year) if you'd like us to look after it.
