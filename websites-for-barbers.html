Why It Matters

Your Instagram gets them interested. Your website gets them booked.

Most barbers rely on Instagram and walk-ins. That works, until a new customer searches "barber near me", can't find your prices or opening hours, and goes to the shop down the road that shows them straight away.

A proper website puts your prices, hours, location and booking in one place, shows up on Google, and works for you even when you're mid-fade and can't answer the phone.
