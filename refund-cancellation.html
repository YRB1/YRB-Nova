Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This page explains how refunds and cancellations work for website projects and the optional hosting & support add-on from YRB Nova, run by Ben, based in Leicestershire, UK. It summarises sections 5 and 11 of our Terms & Conditions.

1. Website projects (Basic, Standard and Premium)

  • Website packages are a one-off, fixed price, paid in full when you book.
  • Your website is delivered after full payment of the one-off amount.
  • For 14 days after delivery, you can request a refund if the website has an irreparable technical error caused by us, meaning a fault we are unable to fix.
  • If you or anyone else makes changes to the website after delivery, refund eligibility no longer applies.
  • Refunds are not given for a change of mind or a change of plans once the project has been booked.

2. Fixing problems first

If something isn't working as it should, tell us as soon as possible at yrbnova@gmail.com. We will always try to fix a technical fault before a refund is considered.

3. Hosting & support add-on (£10 per month or £100 per year)

  • Hosting & support is optional, billed automatically by card: £10 every month or £100 every year, in advance.
  • You can cancel it with one month's notice by email.
  • Amounts already paid are not refunded.
  • When hosting ends, the website will be taken offline.

4. How to ask for a refund or cancel

Email yrbnova@gmail.com with your name, the email you booked with and a short description of the issue. We reply within one business day. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment card.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights under UK law.
