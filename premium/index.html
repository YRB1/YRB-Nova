Questions clinics ask

Before you book a call.

We already have a receptionist.

Good. This covers the hours she isn't there: evenings, weekends, lunch, and when she's already on another line. You choose when calls forward.

Will patients mind talking to an AI?

Callers are told up front they're speaking to an AI assistant, and most simply want a quick answer and a booking. Ring the demo and judge for yourself.

Does it connect to our practice software?

It books straight into Google, Outlook or Apple calendars. It doesn't plug into dental practice-management software yet, so for most clinics it takes the caller's details and sends them to your team to confirm first thing. Nothing gets lost.

What about clinical questions and emergencies?

It never gives clinical advice. Urgent or sensitive calls are transferred to your on-call number, or callers are told to ring NHS 111 or 999 where that's right.

How is patient data handled?

Callers are told the call is recorded. Calls are processed by our voice AI provider under a data processing agreement, and we agree in writing what's kept, and for how long, before going live. See our privacy policy.

Can we keep our number and our website?

You keep your number. If your current website already converts well, we can set up the receptionist on its own instead.
