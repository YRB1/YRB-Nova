Safety & trust

Built to know when a human is needed

VoiceDesk is a receptionist, not a doctor, vet or engineer. It's set up to recognise urgent or sensitive calls and hand them to your team.

  • Urgent calls go to a person. Medical, animal-welfare and safety emergencies are transferred to your on-call team, or callers are told to ring 999 where that's right.
  • UK GDPR-minded. Callers are told they're speaking to an AI and that the call is recorded. Calls are processed by our voice AI provider under a data processing agreement, and we agree what's kept, and for how long, with you before going live.
  • You stay in control. Call recordings, transcripts and summaries are available to you, and you decide what it can and can't say.
  • No surprises. We agree every answer about prices and policies with you first.
