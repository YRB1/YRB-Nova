Five Ways We Support Our Community

Food Support

Weekly Food Parcels

Our founding program provides fresh, nutritious food parcels to families facing hardship across the region — no referral, no means test, and no judgement.

  • Open drop-in every Tuesday and Friday at the community hall
  • Parcels tailored to dietary needs and household size
  • Emergency same-day parcels available for crisis situations
  • Over 400 parcels distributed every month
Volunteer Network

Community Befriending & Support

Local volunteers deliver food, transport, and — just as importantly — company and conversation to elderly and isolated members of our community.

  • Weekly befriending visits for isolated older residents
  • Volunteer-driven transport to medical appointments
  • Companionship calls for those unable to receive visitors
  • 46 active volunteers giving over 300 hours a month
Community Programs

Family Stability Workshops

Long-term projects that help families build stability, not just get by — from budgeting workshops to cookery classes and back-to-work support.

  • Free monthly budgeting and benefits advice clinics
  • Low-cost family cookery classes using food parcel staples
  • Partnerships with local employers for back-to-work support
  • Designed with input from the families who use them
Youth Mentoring

Youth Mentoring Programme

We pair young people aged 11–18 with trained, DBS-checked mentors for a year of regular one-to-one support, building confidence, resilience, and life skills.

  • Weekly one-to-one sessions for a minimum of 12 months
  • All mentors trained, vetted, and supervised by our programmes team
  • Group activity days to build peer confidence and teamwork
  • Currently supporting 34 young people across the region
Winter Appeal

Warm Homes Winter Appeal

Every winter we distribute emergency fuel vouchers and warm packs — blankets, thermal wear, and hot water bottles — to households at risk of going cold.

  • Emergency fuel top-up vouchers for households in crisis
  • Warm packs distributed alongside food parcels each winter
  • Referral partnerships with local GP surgeries and schools
  • Over 200 households supported last winter alone
