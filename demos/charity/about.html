Our Story

About Hopewell Trust

How We Started

From a Kitchen Table to a Community Movement

Hopewell Trust began in 2016 around a kitchen table, when a small group of neighbours started putting together food parcels for families they knew were struggling. What started as a handful of boxes a week has grown into a registered charity supporting hundreds of families across the region every year.

Nine years on, our approach hasn't changed: no red tape, no judgement, and support that meets people where they are — whether that's a food parcel this week, or a mentor for their child over the next year.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

2016

Hopewell Trust Founded

A group of local volunteers began distributing food parcels from a church hall to a handful of families in need.

2017

Registered as a Charity

Demand outgrew our volunteer network, so we formalised as a registered charity and opened our first drop-in centre.

2019

Community Hall Opens

We moved into our current community hall, giving us space for food storage, workshops, and volunteer training.

2020

Emergency COVID-19 Response

We scaled food deliveries fourfold in weeks to reach isolated and vulnerable households during lockdown.

2022

Youth Mentoring Programme Launches

We launched one-to-one mentoring for young people, expanding beyond emergency support into long-term change.

2025

850+ Families Helped in a Single Year

We passed our biggest milestone yet, supporting more families in one year than in our first five combined.
