Treatments / Clear aligners

Straighter teeth, without the metal

Clear aligners are removable, near-invisible trays that move your teeth a little at a time. Most people around you won't even notice you're wearing them.

From £1,995

Book clear aligners Ask a question

What it is

A series of custom-made clear trays, each worn for one to two weeks, gently moving your teeth into place. We plan your whole treatment with a 3D scan so you can see your new smile before you start.

Who it's for

  • Adults and older teens with crowded, gappy or crooked teeth
  • Anyone who had braces as a child and has seen their teeth move back
  • People who want a discreet option for work or social life

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Free smile scan

    A quick 3D scan, no messy moulds, and a chat about what you want to change.

  2. See your new smile

    We show you a digital preview of your result and a fixed, written price.

  3. Wear your aligners

    Swap to a new set every week or two, with check-ins every 6 to 8 weeks.

  4. Keep it straight

    Retainers keep your new smile in place for good.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Does it hurt?

You'll feel some pressure for a day or two with each new set. That's a sign they're working. Most people find it far more comfortable than traditional braces.

How long will it take?

Most cases take between 3 and 12 months. Your dentist will give you a personal estimate at your free consultation.

Can I eat normally?

Yes. You take your aligners out to eat and drink anything other than water, so there are no food restrictions.

Is it available on the NHS?

Cosmetic straightening isn't available on the NHS for adults. Children who meet NHS criteria can be referred for orthodontic assessment.
