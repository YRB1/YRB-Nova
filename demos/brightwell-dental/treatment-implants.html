Treatments / Dental implants

Replace missing teeth for good

A dental implant is the closest thing to a natural tooth. It's fixed in place, looks completely natural and lets you eat, smile and talk with confidence again.

From £2,495

Book dental implants Ask a question

What it is

A small titanium post placed in your jaw where the root of your tooth used to be. Once it has healed, we fit a crown on top that's colour-matched to your other teeth.

Who it's for

  • Anyone missing one or more teeth
  • People tired of loose dentures
  • Patients with healthy gums and enough bone, or who can have bone grafting

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Consultation & 3D scan

    We check your gums and bone and talk through every option, including alternatives.

  2. Your written plan

    A clear timeline and a fixed price before anything starts.

  3. Placing the implant

    A gentle procedure under local anaesthetic, with sedation if you prefer.

  4. Your new tooth

    After healing, usually 3 to 4 months, we fit your custom crown.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Does it hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic, so you shouldn't feel pain. Most people say it was easier than having a tooth out. Sedation is available if you're anxious.

How long do implants last?

With good care and regular check-ups, implants can last for many years, often decades.

Am I suitable?

Most adults with healthy gums are. Smoking and some medical conditions can affect healing, which we'll discuss at your consultation.

Can I have implants on the NHS?

Implants are rarely available on the NHS and only in specific clinical circumstances. We'll explain your NHS and private options.
