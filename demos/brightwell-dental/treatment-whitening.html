A brighter smile, done safely

Coffee, tea and time all leave their mark. Professional whitening lifts stains gently and safely, with results you can see within a couple of weeks.

What it is

Dentist-prescribed whitening gel in custom trays you wear at home, or a faster in-chair treatment. Unlike salon or online kits, it's legal, safe and supervised by a dentist.

Who it's for

  • Adults with healthy teeth and gums
  • Anyone with stains from coffee, tea, red wine or smoking
  • People preparing for a wedding or big event

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Quick check

    We make sure your teeth and gums are healthy enough for whitening.

  2. Custom trays

    We scan your teeth and make trays that fit perfectly.

  3. Whiten at home

    Wear your trays for a short time each day for 2 to 3 weeks.

  4. Keep it bright

    Top-up gel keeps your smile bright for longer.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Is whitening safe?

Yes, when it's done by a dentist. In the UK it's only legal for dentists to provide whitening with effective strength gel, because they check your teeth are healthy first.

Will it make my teeth sensitive?

Some people feel mild, temporary sensitivity. We give you a gel and routine to keep it to a minimum.

How long does it last?

Results usually last a year or more, depending on what you eat and drink. Occasional top-ups keep it fresh.

Will it whiten crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening only works on natural teeth, so we'll talk you through options if you have visible crowns or fillings.
