Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and visit our practices, whatever their needs.

This website

  • Works with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation and browser zoom up to 400%.
  • Uses clear headings, labelled forms, visible focus outlines and good colour contrast.
  • Respects your device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Every image has a text description.

Our practices

Most of our practices have step-free access and ground-floor surgeries. Hearing loops are available at reception, and we're happy to book longer appointments for anyone who needs extra time. Check each practice page for parking and access details.

Tell us if something isn't working

If you have trouble using this site or need information in a different format, call 0115 496 0400 or email hello@brightwell-dental.example. We aim to reply within two working days.
