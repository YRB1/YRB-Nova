Services / Pet dental care

Healthy teeth mean a happier, longer life

Most dogs and cats over three have some dental disease, and they're experts at hiding the pain. A clean mouth can transform how they eat, play and feel.

From £295

Book pet dental care Ask a question

What it is

A thorough clean under anaesthetic to remove tartar above and below the gumline, a full check of every tooth, and gentle removal of any teeth that are causing pain.

Who it's for

  • Pets with bad breath, red gums or brown tartar
  • Dogs and cats who chew on one side or drop food
  • Older pets who haven't had a dental check recently

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Free nurse dental check

    We look at your pet's mouth and give you a written estimate.

  2. Dental day

    A full clean and check under anaesthetic, with dental X-rays where needed.

  3. Home the same day

    With pain relief and soft food tips.

  4. Keep it clean

    Simple home care advice so you don't need another one soon.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Why does my pet need an anaesthetic?

Cleaning under the gumline, where most disease is, can't be done safely while your pet is awake. Anaesthetic also lets us take X-rays and check every tooth.

How do I know if my pet has dental pain?

Look for bad breath, red gums, dropping food, chewing on one side or pawing at the mouth. Many pets show no signs at all, which is why checks matter.

Can I brush my pet's teeth?

Yes, and it's the best thing you can do. Our nurses will show you how, with pet-safe toothpaste.

Is dental care covered by insurance?

Some policies cover dental treatment after an accident or illness. Check your policy, and we're happy to help with claims.
