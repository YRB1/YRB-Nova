Services / Pet Health Plans

All their routine care, in one monthly payment

No more surprise bills for the basics. Our Pet Health Plans cover the routine care your pet needs to stay healthy, and you save money compared with paying separately.

What it is

A monthly plan that includes annual vaccinations, year-round flea and worm treatment, two health checks a year and discounts on other treatment. It isn't insurance, but it works brilliantly alongside it.

Who it's for

  • Dogs, cats and rabbits of any age
  • New puppies and kittens (with a starter plan)
  • Owners who like to know exactly what they'll spend each month

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Choose a plan

    Pick the plan for your pet's species and size.

  2. Sign up online or in clinic

    Set up a simple monthly direct debit.

  3. Care, all year

    Book included treatments whenever they're due.

  4. Save as you go

    Members get 10% off most other treatment.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Is a health plan the same as insurance?

No. A plan covers routine, preventative care. Insurance covers unexpected illness and accidents. Most owners have both.

Can I cancel?

Yes, after 12 months you can cancel at any time. If you cancel early, you just pay the difference for anything you've already used.

Can I use my plan at any clinic?

Yes, at all 9 Hollow Oak clinics.

Does it cover emergencies?

It doesn't cover the cost of emergency treatment, but members get 10% off most treatment, including some emergency care.
