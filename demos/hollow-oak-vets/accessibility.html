Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and visit our clinics, whatever their needs.

This website

  • Works with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation and browser zoom up to 400%.
  • Uses clear headings, labelled forms, visible focus outlines and good colour contrast.
  • Respects your device's reduced-motion setting.
  • Every image has a text description.

Our clinics

All of our clinics have step-free access and accessible parking bays. Assistance dogs are always welcome, and we're happy to arrange quieter appointment times for anyone who needs them.

Tell us if something isn't working

If you have trouble using this site or need information in a different format, call 0117 496 0300 or email hello@hollowoakvets.example. We aim to reply within two working days.
