Services / Neutering

Neutering with care, and with clear prices

It's natural to feel nervous about your pet having an operation. Our team does this every day, with gentle handling, proper pain relief and plenty of updates for you.

From £185

Book neutering Ask a question

What it is

A routine operation (spaying for females, castration for males) that prevents unwanted litters and can reduce the risk of some cancers and behaviour problems. Most pets go home the same day.

Who it's for

  • Cats from 4 months
  • Dogs from 6 months, depending on breed and size
  • Rabbits from around 4 to 6 months

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Pre-op check

    A free consultation to check your pet is fit for surgery and answer your questions.

  2. Drop off in the morning

    Your pet is settled in a quiet, warm kennel by our nurses.

  3. Surgery & recovery

    Monitored throughout by a vet and nurse, with pain relief.

  4. Home the same day

    Plus free post-op checks at 3 and 10 days.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Is neutering painful?

Your pet is fully anaesthetised during the operation and goes home with pain relief. Most are back to their usual selves within a few days.

Will it change my pet's personality?

Your pet will still be your pet. Neutering can calm some hormone-driven behaviours like roaming or marking, but it won't change their character.

Does my pet need a buster collar?

Usually, yes, or a soft recovery suit, to stop them licking the wound. We'll provide one.

Can I stay with my pet?

You'll drop them off in the morning and we'll call you as soon as they're awake, usually by lunchtime.
