FAQs

Common questions

Do I need planning permission?

Many extensions and lofts fall under permitted development, but not all. We check at the first visit and handle the application if you need one.

Is your quote really fixed?

Yes. Our quotes are itemised and fixed. The price only changes if you change the design, and we agree any change in writing before work starts.

Can we stay at home during the build?

Usually, yes. We plan the work in stages so you keep a working kitchen and bathroom for as long as possible.

How soon can you start?

Most projects start 6 to 10 weeks after the quote is accepted, once plans and approvals are in place.
