About Corbel

Family-run, and proud of every wall

Corbel started in 2009 with one van, two brothers and a simple promise: turn up when we say, quote a price we stick to, and leave every site tidy.

Today we're a team of 24 site managers, carpenters, bricklayers and finishers, still family-run, still based in Wigston, and still building almost entirely by recommendation.
