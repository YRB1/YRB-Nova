What it is

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Is laser safe for darker skin?

Yes, with the right laser and settings. Our practitioners are experienced in treating all skin types, and we always patch test first.

How much downtime is there?

It depends on the treatment. Lighter treatments may cause a day of redness; deeper resurfacing can need a few days. We'll plan around your diary.

How many sessions will I need?

Most concerns improve over a course of three sessions, four to six weeks apart.

Can I have laser in summer?

Some treatments, yes, with strict sun protection. Your practitioner will advise the best timing.
