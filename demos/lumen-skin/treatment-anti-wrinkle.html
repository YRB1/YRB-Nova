What it is

A thorough consultation with a doctor, dentist or nurse prescriber to assess your skin, discuss your goals and explain every option. Where a prescription treatment is suitable, it's only prescribed after this consultation, and you'll always have time to think.

Who it's for

  • Adults over 18 bothered by forehead lines, frown lines or crow's feet
  • Anyone who wants a subtle, natural-looking result
  • People who'd like an honest opinion before committing to anything

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Face-to-face consultation

    Your medical history, goals and a full skin assessment with a prescriber.

  2. Your personal plan

    A written plan with prices. Time to think, never pressure.

  3. Treatment

    If suitable, treatment by your prescriber in a private suite.

  4. Two-week review

    A complimentary review to make sure you're delighted.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Will I look "frozen"?

Not with us. Our signature is subtle: we aim for softened lines and natural movement, so friends notice you look well, not that you've had something done.

Who will treat me?

Every consultation and treatment is carried out by a registered doctor, dentist or nurse prescriber with advanced aesthetic training.

How long do results last?

Typically three to four months, varying from person to person. Your prescriber will talk you through what to expect.

Is there any downtime?

Most people return to work straight away. You may notice slight redness for an hour or two.
