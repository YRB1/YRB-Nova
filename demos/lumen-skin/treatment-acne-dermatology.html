Book acne & dermatology Ask a question

What it is

An appointment with a consultant dermatologist to diagnose and treat conditions including acne, rosacea, pigmentation, eczema and suspicious moles, combining medical treatment with in-clinic therapies where helpful.

Who it's for

  • Adults and teenagers (with a parent) with acne or acne scarring
  • Anyone with rosacea, redness or pigmentation
  • People who want a mole or skin lesion checked quickly

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Dermatologist consultation

    A thorough assessment with a consultant, usually within a week.

  2. Diagnosis & plan

    A clear explanation and a written treatment plan.

  3. Treatment

    Prescriptions, in-clinic therapies, or both.

  4. Follow-up

    Regular reviews until your skin is where you want it.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly, and we'll write to your GP afterwards if you'd like us to.

How quickly can I be seen?

Most clients see a consultant dermatologist within a week, often sooner.

Can you prescribe medication?

Yes. Our consultants can prescribe where appropriate, including specialist acne treatments with the right monitoring.

What happens if a mole looks concerning?

We'll explain our findings straight away and arrange fast removal and testing, or an urgent referral, as needed.
