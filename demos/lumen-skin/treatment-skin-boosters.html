Treatments / Skin boosters

The glow you get after a really good holiday

Skin boosters hydrate your skin from within, improving texture, fine lines and that tired, dull look that no moisturiser quite reaches.

From £295

Book skin boosters Ask a question

What it is

Micro-injections of a hydrating gel spread evenly across the face, neck or hands, drawing in moisture and supporting your skin's natural renewal. Results build gradually over a course of treatments.

Who it's for

  • Anyone with dry, dull or tired-looking skin
  • Fine lines on the face, neck or décolletage
  • Clients preparing for a wedding or special event

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Skin consultation

    A detailed analysis of your skin and goals.

  2. Numbing & prep

    A soothing numbing cream for comfort.

  3. Treatment

    Around 30 minutes, with minimal discomfort.

  4. Course & glow

    Usually two or three sessions, a month apart.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

When will I see results?

Many clients notice a fresher look within a week, with the best results after a full course.

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream first, so most clients describe it as mild and very manageable.

Is there downtime?

You may have small bumps for 24 to 48 hours. We recommend avoiding make-up until the next day.

How long does it last?

Results typically last six to nine months, with a single maintenance session to keep the glow.
