About Brightside

We started Brightside to be the cleaning company we couldn't find: reliable, friendly and genuinely careful with people's homes.

From one van to 30 cleaners

Brightside began in 2017 with one van, one mop and a handful of neighbours who needed help. We did things properly: turned up on time, paid attention to the details, and treated every home like our own.

Word spread. Today our team of 30 cleaners looks after more than 300 homes and offices across Leicestershire, but we still run things the same way. Every new customer gets a personal call, and every cleaner is someone we'd trust in our own homes.
