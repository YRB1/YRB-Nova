FAQs

Good questions, clear answers

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not at all. Most customers leave a key or code with us. Every cleaner is DBS-checked and fully insured.

Do you bring your own products?

Yes. We bring all eco-friendly products and professional equipment. If you'd prefer we use your own, just let us know.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean the area for free. No arguments.

Can I cancel or skip a clean?

Of course. There are no contracts. Skip, pause or cancel with 48 hours' notice at no charge.
