About us

Family-run since 2002.

G&K Developments Limited is a family-run construction company based in Clay Cross, operating since 2002. We take on jobs of any size, from a small garden wall to a housing or commercial development, and every project is overseen from start to finish.

We value honesty, openness and turning up when we say we will. Our staff are punctual, polite and professional, and we aim to finish every project on time and at the price quoted.
