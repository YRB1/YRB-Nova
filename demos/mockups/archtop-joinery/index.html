About

A proper workshop, a proper team.

Archtop Joinery is a traditionally skilled joinery workshop in Wirksworth, specialising in sash windows, doors, staircases and bespoke kitchens. Our experienced craftsmen work with a wide range of materials and finishes.

We work with homeowners, building contractors, local authorities, property developers, building managers and architects, with an installation service that leaves nothing to chance.
