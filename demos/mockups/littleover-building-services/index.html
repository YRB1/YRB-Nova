About

A building business built on reputation.

Since 1985 Littleover Building Services has built homes, extensions and conversions across Derby, Derbyshire and the East Midlands. We are members of the Federation of Master Builders and TrustMark, and Constructionline certified, with warranties and insurance backed guarantees.

Our own estimators price the job in detail and our Buying Department chases competitive prices. Once work starts, your Contracts Manager visits every day to keep things on programme and is always on hand for updates.
