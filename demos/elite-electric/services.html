Our Services

Full Electrical Services For Homes & Businesses

From a single faulty socket to a full rewire, our certified team covers the complete range of domestic and commercial electrical work.

What We Do

The Full Service Catalogue

01

Full & Partial Rewiring

Whether you're renovating a period property or bringing an older home up to modern safety standards, we carry out full and partial rewires with minimal disruption. We plan the work in stages, protect your floors and walls, and make good behind us so the job looks tidy, not just functional. Every rewire is tested and signed off with the correct certification.

From £1,800

02

Fuse Boxes & Consumer Unit Upgrades

Old fuse boxes are one of the most common fire and safety risks in UK homes. We replace outdated boards with modern, RCD-protected consumer units, fix wiring faults we find along the way, and explain exactly what's changed and why in plain English before we leave.

From £350

03

EV Charger Installation

We supply and install home EV charge points from all major manufacturers, sized correctly for your property's supply and future-proofed where possible for a second vehicle. Every installation includes load calculations, a dedicated circuit, and full manufacturer warranty registration.

From £650

04

Electrical Inspections & EICR Certificates

An Electrical Installation Condition Report gives you a clear, documented picture of your property's electrical safety. We're regularly instructed by landlords, letting agents and homeowners buying or selling, and we can usually turn around the paperwork within 48 hours of the visit.

From £120

05

Lighting Design & Installation

From simple room rewires to layered lighting schemes with dimmer zones, feature pendants and outdoor lighting, we help plan a layout that works for the space and then install it properly — including LED retrofits that cut your running costs.

From £180

06

Smart Home & Automation Wiring

We install the wiring and infrastructure behind smart lighting, heating controls, video doorbells and whole-home automation systems, working alongside your preferred smart-home platform so everything talks to everything else reliably.

From £220

07

Landlord & Commercial Safety Certificates

Landlords and commercial property owners have specific legal obligations around electrical safety. We provide EICRs, PAT testing, and emergency lighting certification, and we can set up a rolling schedule so you never miss a renewal date.

From £95
