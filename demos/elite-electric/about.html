Our Story

Built On Reliability, Not Shortcuts

Elite Electric was founded in 2008 by James Whitfield, a qualified electrician who was tired of seeing homeowners get burned by unreliable tradespeople and vague, after-the-fact invoices. The idea was simple: turn up when you say you will, quote a fair price upfront, and do the job properly the first time.

Almost two decades later, that's still the whole business model. We've grown from a single van to a full team covering domestic, landlord and commercial electrical work across the region, but every job still gets the same upfront quote, the same tidy workmanship, and the same paperwork at the end.

Every electrician who joins the team is NICEIC assessed before they ever visit a customer on their own, and every job is backed by our workmanship guarantee.
