Treatments / Sports massage

Loosen up. Recover properly.

Whether you're training for a marathon or just sitting at a desk all day, a good sports massage takes the tension out and gets you moving freely again.

What it is

A firm, focused massage from a qualified sports massage therapist, targeting the muscles that are tight or overworked. We can combine it with stretching and simple advice to stop the tightness coming back.

Who it's for

  • Runners, cyclists and gym-goers in training
  • Anyone with tight shoulders, neck or back from desk work
  • People recovering after an event or a hard block of training

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Quick check-in

    Where it's tight, what you've been doing, any injuries.

  2. Targeted massage

    Deep tissue work focused on the areas that need it.

  3. Stretch & release

    Assisted stretching to lock in the gains.

  4. Aftercare tips

    Simple stretches to keep you loose between sessions.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Will it hurt?

It's firm, but it should never be more than you're comfortable with. Tell your therapist and they'll adjust.

How often should I have one?

In heavy training, every two to four weeks works well. Otherwise, whenever you feel tight.

Should I have one before a race?

A light massage two to three days before is ideal. Save deep work for after the event.

Is it physio?

It's complementary. If your therapist spots an injury, they'll suggest a physio assessment.
