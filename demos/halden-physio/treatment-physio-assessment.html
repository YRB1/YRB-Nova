What it is

A 45-minute appointment with a chartered physiotherapist. We take your history, test movement, strength and joints, give you a clear diagnosis and start hands-on treatment, then send you home with a simple exercise plan.

Who it's for

  • Anyone with a new or ongoing ache, pain or injury
  • People who've been told to "rest it" and it hasn't helped
  • Anyone who wants a proper diagnosis before spending more

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Your story

    What happened, what hurts and what you want to get back to.

  2. Movement tests

    Strength, range and joint tests to find the cause, not just the pain.

  3. Diagnosis & first treatment

    A clear explanation, then hands-on treatment straight away.

  4. Your home plan

    Two or three exercises sent to your phone, with videos.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Do I need a GP referral?

No. You can book directly. If we think you need a scan or a GP appointment, we'll tell you and write a letter.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable you can move in. Shorts for knee or ankle problems, a vest top for shoulders.

Can I use my health insurance?

Yes. We work with most major insurers. Bring your authorisation number and we'll bill them directly.

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need three to five. We'll give you an honest estimate at your first visit.
