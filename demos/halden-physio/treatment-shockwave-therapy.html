Treatments / Shockwave therapy

For the pain that just won't shift

Tendon problems can drag on for months. Shockwave therapy is a well-researched option for stubborn cases, used alongside the right exercise plan.

From £70

Book shockwave therapy Ask a question

What it is

Short pulses of sound waves are delivered to the painful tendon through a handheld device, which helps kick-start healing and reduce pain. Each session takes around 15 minutes and is usually given weekly for three to five weeks.

Who it's for

  • Plantar fasciitis (heel pain)
  • Achilles and patellar tendon pain
  • Tennis and golfer's elbow
  • Shoulder tendon problems that haven't settled

The process

What happens, step by step

  1. Assessment first

    We confirm shockwave is right for you.

  2. Treatment

    Around 15 minutes per session, with no needles.

  3. Exercise plan

    Loading exercises to strengthen the tendon.

  4. Review

    Progress check after three sessions.

FAQs

Your questions, answered

Does it hurt?

It can be uncomfortable, but we adjust the intensity so it stays tolerable. Most sessions take around 15 minutes.

How many sessions will I need?

Usually three to five, a week apart.

Is it suitable for everyone?

Not for everyone. We'll check your health at your assessment, as it isn't used during pregnancy or with some medical conditions.

Where is it available?

At our Leicester and Nottingham clinics, after an assessment at any Halden clinic.
