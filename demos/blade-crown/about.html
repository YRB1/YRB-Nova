Our Story

Built On Word Of Mouth

Blade & Crown started as a single chair and a waiting list of friends. Word spread, the waiting list grew, and in 2019 we opened our doors on the High Street.

We've kept the same approach ever since: proper consultations, no rushing, and a relaxed shop where you actually look forward to your next cut. Most of our customers have been with us for years, and many now bring their kids too.
