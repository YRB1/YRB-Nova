Our Story

Two Decades Of Local Expertise

Meridian Property was founded on a simple idea: that selling a home deserves the same care and attention as buying one.

Who We Are

Built On Relationships, Not Transactions

Meridian Property was founded over twenty years ago by a small group of local agents who were frustrated with how impersonal the industry had become. Rather than chase volume across dozens of postcodes, we made a deliberate choice to go deep in a handful of areas we actually know — the schools, the commute times, the neighbours, the little things that never show up on a listing sheet.

That decision has shaped everything since. Every member of our team lives locally, and every sale is treated with the same level of care whether it's a first flat or a country house. We built our reputation on straight answers and honest valuations, and we've kept it that way as the agency has grown.
